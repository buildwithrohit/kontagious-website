The Kontagious Journey to Success

April 19, 2025 · By Sunil Kumar

A music evening to start

Picture a music evening at San José State University where graduate students meet the local community, trade stories and find friends. Nights like this are where many first-year students realize they are not alone.

A familiar face at the airport

After registering, students join a virtual meeting where we help them prepare for packing, the challenges ahead and immigration. On arrival, a Kontagious team member greets them with a welcome bag of essentials and helps them get safely home.

That welcome matters most for students arriving without family.

Community from day one

Welcome dinners, game nights, beach outings, music evenings and hikes give students places to build friendships and find support.

More than a degree

We see the years in the U.S. as a chance to grow personally, professionally and relationally, with introductions to local families and professionals.

Practical help that lasts

Students get help with American culture, driver's licenses, professional networking, resumes, interview preparation and internships that can lead to leadership roles.

Future leaders

We see every student as a future leader. If you would like to be part of that, join the community.
