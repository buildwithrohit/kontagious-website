About us

Kindness that opens our communities

We believe intentional kindness becomes the hope and light that opens our communities to families relocating from across the globe.

Kontagious uses unconventional, entrepreneurial approaches to help newcomers feel at home in the multicultural mosaic of our cities. We develop technologies, incubate startups, make space for art and music, and enrich marriage and family life.

Simple acts of love lead our communities toward new waves of inclusion. This is Kontagious.
