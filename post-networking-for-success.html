Networking for Success

March 18, 2025 · By John Christian

Building connections over great food, guided by experienced seniors ready to share their wisdom.

Networking is a lifestyle and an essential key to professional success and personal growth, especially for international students in the U.S. Kontagious helps students connect with business leaders, executives and professionals across the Bay Area.

Leadership development

We help students grow into leaders from the start. Programs build networking, public speaking, interpersonal and cross-cultural communication skills.

Adapting and connecting

The student journey begins with communication skills and confidence, then links students with leaders in their fields.

Leadership from within

Students take on small leadership roles, such as leading discussions or helping run camps. Leadership begins from within.

Camps, workshops and events

Our Tahoe and Yosemite camps offer mentorship, training and community. Workshops cover resume writing, interviewing and professional development.

Two to three times a year we host Rare Leadership events, inspired by the book of the same name, on emotional resilience, character and influence. Smaller training sessions run all year, and our volunteers are former students who understand what today's students face.

Take the next step

New chapter students are invited to grow in leadership, build friendships and reach out to directors or leaders with questions. Welcome to Kontagious!
