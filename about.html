About us

Where strangers become family

Kontagious encourages ordinary people to practice intentional kindness toward those from other cultures, so newcomers to our cities feel at home.

Our mission

Kontagious inspires ordinary people to embrace a lifestyle of intentional kindness toward those outside their own culture, creating a welcoming home away from home for people new to our cities. It all begins with a smile.

We use unconventional and entrepreneurial approaches to help immigrants find their place in the multicultural mosaic of our communities: developing technologies, incubating startups, making space for art and music, and enriching marriage and family life.

Meet our team

John Christian

CEO

A social entrepreneur with more than 30 years of community transformation work in India and the USA. He founded the Unwind Center in Chennai and focuses on developing leaders and building grassroots movements.

Carol Christian

Director, Community Development

American-born with Indian roots, Carol mentors young people by modeling positive behavior and shares practical strategies for developing leaders of character and compassion.

Bob Thomas

Board Member

A former college wrestler and national champion who went on to earn a Master of Divinity. He has spent decades mentoring youth, students and adults and connects Bay Area business leaders with new immigrants.

Ken Raasch

Board Member

About 30 years in art, entertainment, branding, retail and licensing. Ken founded Immersive Artistry, leads Creative Brands Group and founded the nonprofit Mercy Ventures.

Cathy Smith

Board Member

CEO and President of ELREPCO, which serves technology clients in semiconductors, storage and power solutions. She supports Bay Area organizations including YoungLife and the San Jose Family Shelter.

Samuel Chacko

Virendra Vase

Maxine Chicoine-Bagoye
