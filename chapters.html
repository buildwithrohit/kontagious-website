Student chapters

Student-led, mentor-backed

Kontagious chapters give international students a community on campus, built and led by students.

San José State University

The SJSU chapter is an officially recognized student organization. Each chapter has a student leadership team (a President, several Vice Presidents, a Treasurer and other members) with mentorship from the senior Kontagious team.

Chapters focus on leadership development, campus culture and kindness toward newcomers, through leadership chats, breakfast meetings and a yearly Celebration of Leadership where presidents hand over their roles.
