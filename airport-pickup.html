Airport pickup

Your first stop in America should feel welcoming

A free, volunteer-run service for new students at San José State University and Santa Clara University.

Who it's for

Pickup is for new, incoming students enrolled at SJSU or Santa Clara University who are flying into SFO or SJC for the first time to begin their studies.

It is not available to continuing students or to students already in the U.S. who are relocating or returning.

Typical pickup windows: SJSU from July 15 to August 15, and SCU in July. Dates are confirmed each year.

Sign up for pickup

How it works

  1. Submit your arrival information through the sign-up form.
  2. Receive a confirmation email. There is no need to resubmit.
  3. Join a required Zoom call so we can confirm your identity and share arrival tips on immigration, baggage claim and meeting points.
  4. Get added to a WhatsApp group with others arriving around the same time.
  5. Meet a Kontagious welcomer at the airport. We track your flight, even if it is delayed.
  6. A volunteer drives you to your accommodation. Please confirm your address and tell us right away if it changes.

Information is verified, including admission details. If someone intentionally gives false information, they may be responsible for any resulting costs.

Welcome dinners

Free transportation is included and anyone can attend, even without using airport pickup. Registration is required through a separate link we send after you sign up for pickup.
