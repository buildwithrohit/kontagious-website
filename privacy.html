Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Kontagious (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

Information you send us. If you email us or use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message. If you sign up for an event, program or airport pickup, the information you enter goes to us through a Google Form and is governed by Google's privacy policy in addition to this one.

Website visit data. Our website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to you, run events and programs, match students with volunteers and hosts, send acknowledgements and tax receipts for gifts, and keep our community informed. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with the volunteers and partners who need it to run a program, with service providers who help us operate (for example, payment and form providers), or when required by law.

Donations

Gifts made through GoFundMe, Zelle, your bank or a brokerage are processed by those providers under their own policies. We keep a record of gifts so we can issue receipts and meet our legal obligations as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

Children

Our programs serve university students and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@kontagious.org.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date above.
