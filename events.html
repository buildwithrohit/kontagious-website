RARE Leadership in the Workplace

When: Started July 24, 2026
Where: John & Carol's residence

Rapid AI advances are changing the workplace faster than most people can keep up with, and young professionals face real stress, anxiety, burnout and loneliness about their future.

This six-week program, based on the book Rare Leadership, teaches four uncommon habits for staying relationally grounded under pressure and bringing stability to an organization.
