Get involved

Join the movement

Kontagious is a welcoming community in California for people building friendships and growing together.

Student Chapter

A graduate student group that hikes, camps, shares meals and offers academic and professional development.

Pickleball Club

A community and leadership program that focuses on character rather than competition.

Midweek Hiking Initiative

Weekly hikes that build skills such as pitching ideas, setting boundaries and living by core values.

Alumni & Volunteer Team

Volunteers drive, lead seminars and welcome international students at airport pickups.

Resource & Leadership Team

Professionals share expertise, such as entrepreneurship and finance, and help organize networking events.

Donor Partnership

Monthly gifts starting at $25 support the mission all year.

Mentoring & Tech Team

Mentor graduate students and alumni, or contribute your skills to our technology work.

Community Service

Trunk-or-treat events and help preparing for university orientations and airport pickups.

Tell us which team sounds like you and we will point you to the right person. You can also reach us on Instagram or Facebook.
